§1

The measurement gap is not your imagination

two audiences, one disease
Shipping AI features?

The dashboard says "value." The formula says otherwise.

Microsoft's Copilot dashboard computes "assisted value" as every action × a flat 6 minutes × a default rate of

$72 / hour

— and the number silently undercounted for two months before anyone noticed. Unfalsifiable numbers can't look wrong.

Receipt: Microsoft Learn, Copilot analytics methodology

Rolling out AI tools internally?

The team feels faster. The clock disagrees.

In a randomized trial, experienced developers using AI assistance were

19% slower

— while estimating they were 20% faster. Nearly every corporate "hours saved" claim rests on exactly that kind of self-report.

Receipt: METR randomized controlled trial, 2025

39%of companies can attribute any profit impact to AI — McKinsey, State of AI
>40%of agentic AI projects predicted canceled by 2027, "unclear business value" leading — Gartner

§3

Five commands, one honest loop

design → audit → instrument → connect → scorecard
1

/actuals:design — the interview

What does the AI do? Who benefits? And the load-bearing question: what will you do differently at different values of this metric? Out comes a versioned metrics/MEASUREMENT.md — formulas, owners, guardrails, and a list of vanity metrics you pre-commit to never using.

### OM-1: Deflection-without-reopen rate
- Formula: strict deflections / all eligible tickets, weekly
- Baseline: 0% (pre-launch) · Target: ≥25% by 2026-10
- Confidence: plausible ← proven | plausible | assumed
2

/actuals:audit — the reckoning

Point it at dashboards, tracking plans, SQL, or the ROI deck. Findings cite the catalog, quote your artifact, carry published receipts, and always ship a fix.

### 2. VM-02 · Minutes-times-Wage Dollarization — critical
- Where: dashboard-export.csv:4 — "1,240 hrs × $72/hr"
- Receipt: [MSFT-COPILOT] flat-multiplier value math
- Fix: cost per resolved ticket; constants live in §2, re-measured
3

/actuals:instrument — make it real

Events with typed constants, SQL/dbt models implementing each formula verbatim, a pre-launch baseline snapshot (so the claims ledger can ever pay out), and an eval harness that reports judge–human agreement next to every score.

4

/actuals:connect — meet your stack where it is

Vetted MCP configs for the tools you already run — merged into your project non-destructively, secrets stay as ${ENV_VAR} placeholders you export yourself.

5

/actuals:scorecard — the shareable truth

A self-contained dashboard.html: metrics vs baselines and targets, tripwires, open findings, the claims ledger. Renders recorded values only — and says so in its footer.

§4

The anti-pattern catalog

stable IDs — cite them like CVEs
VM-01 · CRITICAL

Self-Reported Time Savings

"Devs report saving 11 hrs/week." Perception inverts reality — the METR trial's participants were 19% slower while feeling 20% faster.

→ measure handle time from system data, or drop the claim
VM-02 · CRITICAL

Minutes-times-Wage Dollarization

Actions × minutes-per-action × $/hour. Every constant is an assumption wearing a number's costume — and it can break silently for months.

→ dollarize only through real unit costs with re-measured constants
VM-06 · WARNING

Denominator-Free Count

"10,000 AI conversations!" Out of how many tickets? A numerator without a denominator is a press release, not a metric.

→ share of eligible volume, fixed window, cohort trend

Practicing what it preaches

Actuals ships with its own measurement spec, written with its own method. GitHub stars and installs are listed there under §6 — Vanity Metrics We Will Not Use (VM-03: adoption-as-impact). Its claims ledger commits to never claiming it improved your metrics without your baselines. There is no telemetry in the plugin, and this page loads zero external resources and runs no analytics — not even fonts.
